Hands-on Practice
tep 1: Turn off auto-commit

Modify your consumer config:

'auto.offset.reset': 'earliest',
'enable.auto.commit': False

tep 2: Manually commit after processing

Inside your consumer loop:

consumer.commit(asynchronous=False)


Then — crash your consumer after a few messages, restart it, and watch:

Without manual commit sages replay.

With manual commit umer resumes correctly.

tep 3: Observe behavior

Print offset using msg.offset()

Print partition using msg.partition()

Note how offsets advance as messages are read.
